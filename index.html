How to play

Drive into the distance. In Traffic, points come from near misses — chain them for a multiplier, but one crash ends the run. Flash your lights and slower cars pull over. In Zen there is nothing to hit.

Keyboard

  • WS throttle / brake · AD steer
  • Space handbrake · EQ shift · G auto / manual
  • L lights · F flash (hold) · C camera · K sky
  • R respawn · M mute · Esc pause

Gamepad

  • Left stick steer · triggers brake / throttle · RBLB shift

Garage

  • G generate · M mutate · Z undo · drag to orbit the car

Phones support tilt steering — turn it on in Settings, under Driving.
